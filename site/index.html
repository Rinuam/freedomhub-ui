Chapter I · The Craft

Inside the chamber.

Four disciplines, one loop. This is the work, in the order the wafer sees it.

I

Deposit

First, you build.

Atom by atom, layer by layer. Sputtered, evaporated, or grown one atomic layer per cycle — thickness, stress, uniformity and step coverage are all decided in seconds.

PVD · ALD · CVD — GROWTH ~1 Å PER ALD CYCLE

II

Remove

Then, you carve.

Ions accelerate across the plasma sheath and cut vertical trenches through the stack. Seven knobs decide whether the profile is straight or ruined.

RIE · ICP-RIE · ALE — ANISOTROPIC PROFILES

III

Prove

Then, you prove it.

Ellipsometry, XRR, AFM, SEM. Measuring what you made — and knowing when the measurement, not the process, is what’s wrong.

THICKNESS 42.3 NM · UNIFORMITY ±1.8%

IV

Repeat

Sixty times over.

A modern chip stacks and etches films dozens upon dozens of times. Someone has to know how every one of those layers behaves.

DEPOSIT · ETCH · PROVE — AGAIN, AND AGAIN

Chapter III · The Gap

What school asks. What the fab asks.

In the classroom

  • Derive the Arrhenius expression
  • State the mean free path equation
  • Define anisotropy and selectivity
  • Calculate sputtering yield
  • Describe a normal distribution

On the fab floor

  • Your film is 8% thicker at the wafer edge — why, and what do you change first?
  • Base pressure won’t recover after a chamber open — where do you look?
  • The trench is undercut and sloped — which knob, which direction?
  • Etch rate dropped 20% overnight with no recipe change — diagnose it.
  • Cpk is 1.02 — explain to management what that means.

Both columns are real. Only one of them gets you hired, keeps the line running, and gets you promoted.

Chapter V · Careers

Three doors into the same room.

Getting in

A degree in physics, materials, chemical or electrical engineering, and you want a process role. We teach the tool intuition and the troubleshooting questions the interview will actually test.

Moving up

Already in a fab. You want seniority, the harder modules, and the command of statistics and root cause that promotion runs on.

Switching across

From displays, photovoltaics, coatings or academic cleanrooms. Vacuum and plasma experience transfers further than people think.
